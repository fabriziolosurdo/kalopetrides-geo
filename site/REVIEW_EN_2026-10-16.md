## BLOCKER (wrong fact or rule violation)

### B1 — Wrong tax-residency rule (contradicts the law and the tax page)
- File: `service-immigration.md`, section "Digital Nomad Visa"
- Text: "And more than 183 days in Cyprus in a tax year makes you a Cyprus tax resident unless you are tax resident elsewhere."
- Problem: Section 2 of the Income Tax Law (CyLaw, fetched) says anyone who stays more than 183 days is resident, with no exception. Being tax resident elsewhere was a condition of the old 60-day rule, and the reform removed it. `service-tax.md` states the law correctly, so the two pages contradict each other.
- Fix: "More than 183 days in Cyprus in a tax year makes you Cyprus tax resident under section 2 of the Income Tax Law. Your home country may also treat you as resident, in which case the double tax treaty decides."

### M6 — Forced-heirship rule stated inconsistently and incompletely
- File: `service-wills.md`
- FAQ: "Where a spouse and children survive … one quarter …; where a spouse and a parent survive but no children, one half"
- Body: "where the deceased leaves a spouse and a child …; where the deceased leaves a spouse or a parent but no child …"
- Cap. 195 s.41 (CyLaw, fetched): one quarter where there is a spouse and child, **or a child/descendant without a spouse**; one half where there is a "spouse, father, or mother" and no descendants. The FAQ's "spouse **and** a parent" is wrong in its conditions, and both versions leave out the child-only case.
- Fix (both places): "Where the deceased leaves a child or descendant (with or without a spouse), the disposable portion may not exceed one quarter of the net estate; where the deceased leaves a spouse or a parent but no descendants, one half; otherwise the whole estate is disposable."
