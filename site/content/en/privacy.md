Kalopetrides Law LLC is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why and on what legal basis we process it, with whom we share it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It applies to clients and prospective clients, to persons connected with the companies and entities we act for, to suppliers and to visitors of this website.

This policy is separate from, and in addition to, the duty of confidentiality we owe to clients as lawyers. The terms of your engagement with the firm set out further details.

## Who is the data controller

The controller is **Kalopetrides Law LLC**, Panayioteio Megaro, Gladstonos 1, 2nd floor, Office 205, 6023 Larnaca, Cyprus. Email: info@kalopetrideslaw.com. Phone: +357 24 668667.

We process personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR) and the Cyprus Law 125(I)/2018 providing for the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and for the free movement of such data.

## What personal data we collect

Depending on our relationship with you, we may collect:

- **Contact information:** name, job title, postal, home and business addresses, telephone and mobile numbers, email address.
- **Identification and compliance information:** copies of identity documents and other information we are legally required to collect, such as "know your client" information, information on the source of funds, sanctions and restrictive-measures checks, and information about relevant litigation.
- **Payment data:** information necessary to issue invoices and process payments, including bank details.
- **Business details:** information about your business that we process as part of our instructions, or that you provide voluntarily.
- **Statutory register information:** information about you arising from an office or interest you hold in a company, partnership, trust or other entity to which we or our affiliated company TrustGate Corporate Services Ltd provide services (a "Third-Party Entity").
- **Preferences:** information about your preferences, where relevant to the services we provide.
- **Publicly available information:** information from public registers and databases used for compliance checks.
- **Special categories of data:** in limited cases, for example information about health in a personal injury or family matter, which we process only where necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims, or with your explicit consent.
- **Website data:** the information you enter in the contact form (name, email, company, message) and technical data generated by your visit, as described under "Website" below.

## When we collect your personal data

- When you or your organisation seek or receive our services.
- When you make an enquiry through this website, by email, by phone, by WhatsApp or Telegram, or in person.
- When a Third-Party Entity engages us and you hold an office or interest in it.
- When you or your organisation provide services to us.
- From third parties, such as public authorities, public registers, courts, other lawyers, banks or credit-reference and compliance-screening providers, where this is necessary for our work or required by law.

## Why we process your data and on what legal basis

We process personal data for the following purposes:

- **To provide legal and corporate services** and to manage our relationship with you, including communication, invoicing, accounting and audit (legal basis: performance of a contract, or steps taken at your request before entering into one).
- **To comply with legal obligations**, in particular anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing obligations applying to lawyers and corporate service providers in Cyprus, sanctions screening, tax and accounting rules, and orders of courts and authorities (legal basis: legal obligation).
- **To establish, exercise or defend legal claims**, for us or for our clients (legal basis: legitimate interests, and, for special categories of data, Article 9(2)(f) GDPR).
- **To keep you informed** about matters relevant to your role in a Third-Party Entity (legal basis: legitimate interests).
- **To run, secure and improve our services, communications and this website** (legal basis: legitimate interests).
- **To send you legal updates or invitations**, only where you have consented or where we are otherwise permitted to do so (legal basis: consent; you may withdraw it at any time).

## With whom we share your data

- **Third-Party Entities** for which we act, for administrative or billing purposes connected with your office or interest.
- **TrustGate Corporate Services Ltd**, our affiliated corporate services company, where your matter involves company administration.
- **Service providers** that process data on our behalf under contract and confidentiality obligations: IT and hosting providers, compliance-screening providers, accountants and auditors, translators, and the provider of our website contact form (see "Website").
- **Courts, public authorities, regulators and law-enforcement bodies**, where required by law or necessary for the conduct of a matter, including the Registrar of Companies, the Land Registry, the Civil Registry and Migration Department, the Tax Department and banks.
- **Other lawyers, notaries, experts and counterparties**, where necessary for the conduct of your matter, including lawyers in other countries in cross-border matters.

Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we rely on an adequacy decision of the European Commission or on appropriate safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses, or on a derogation permitted by the GDPR, for example where the transfer is necessary for the performance of your contract or for legal claims.

## Website

**Contact form.** The contact form on this website is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form-handling service that acts as our processor. When you submit the form, the data you enter (name, email address, company, message) and technical data such as your IP address are transmitted to Web3Forms, which forwards the message to our email address. Web3Forms stores submissions on its infrastructure for a limited period and uses spam-prevention services; its own privacy policy is available at [web3forms.com/privacy](https://web3forms.com/privacy). If you prefer not to use the form, you can write to us directly at info@kalopetrideslaw.com.

**Map.** The contact page can display a map provided by Google Maps. The map is loaded only after you click to show it. When it loads, your browser connects to Google's servers and Google may set cookies and process your IP address and usage data under its own privacy policy. Until you click, no data is sent to Google.

**Cookies and technical data.** This website is a static site and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It stores only what is necessary to remember your choice regarding embedded content such as the map. The hosting provider's servers record technical data about each visit (IP address, date and time, pages requested, browser type) in server logs for security and operational purposes, on the basis of our legitimate interest in running the site securely.

**Messaging apps.** If you contact us through WhatsApp or Telegram, the provider of that service processes your data under its own terms and privacy policy.

## How long we keep your data

We keep personal data for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and as required by law. For client files this includes the period during which claims could be brought in connection with the matter and the retention periods imposed on lawyers and corporate service providers by anti-money-laundering, tax and professional rules. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

## How we protect your data

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including secure systems, restricted access to files, confidentiality obligations for staff and associates, and contractual obligations for service providers.

## Data you give us about other people

If you provide us with personal data about other people, for example family members, employees, directors or counterparties, you must make sure that you are entitled to do so and that those people are aware of this privacy policy.

## Can you refuse to provide personal data

You may withhold personal data, but in many cases we will then be unable to act for you, because we are required by law to identify our clients and to carry out compliance checks before and during an engagement.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
- have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed;
- have your data erased, where the conditions of the GDPR are met;
- restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
- receive the data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller, where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

Some of these rights are limited by our professional duty of confidentiality and by legal obligations, including anti-money-laundering rules. To exercise a right, write to info@kalopetrideslaw.com; we may ask you to prove your identity before we respond.

## Complaints

If you believe that we have processed your personal data unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in Cyprus, the **Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection** ([www.dataprotection.gov.cy](https://www.dataprotection.gov.cy/)), or with the supervisory authority of the EU Member State in which you live or work. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first.

## Keeping your data up to date

Please tell us at info@kalopetrideslaw.com of any changes to your personal data so that our records remain accurate.
