## How it works in Cyprus

Cyprus taxes on a residence basis. The first question for every client is therefore whether they, or their company, are Cyprus tax resident, because that decides which income Cyprus taxes and whether SDC applies at all.

### Tax residency of individuals: the 183-day and 60-day rules

Section 2 of the Income Tax Law 118(I)/2002 gives two routes. An individual who stays in Cyprus for more than 183 days in total in a tax year is resident. Alternatively, an individual is resident under the 60-day rule if, in the same tax year, all of the following are met: they do not stay in any other single state for more than 183 days in total; they stay in Cyprus for at least 60 days; they carry on a business in Cyprus, are employed in Cyprus or hold an office in a company that is Cyprus tax resident at any time during the year; and they keep a permanent home in Cyprus that they own or rent. The law adds that the business, employment or office condition is not met if it is terminated during that year.

The consolidated text in October 2026 no longer contains the earlier condition that the person must not be tax resident in any other state. That is a change made by the reform. It does not mean a home-country tax authority will agree: Germany, Austria, the Netherlands and Poland apply their own residence tests and the treaty tie-breaker decides a conflict. The firm looks at both sides before advising a client to rely on the 60-day rule.

### Tax residency of companies

A company is Cyprus tax resident if its control and management are exercised in Cyprus or if it is incorporated in Cyprus under the Companies Law, unless a double tax treaty provides otherwise. A company that moves its registered office or seat to Cyprus is treated as incorporated here. A Cyprus company run entirely from abroad is therefore still Cyprus resident under Cyprus law, while the other country may claim residence as well.

### Corporate income tax: 15% from the 2026 tax year

The Second Schedule of the Income Tax Law provides that a company is taxed at 15% on every euro of taxable income. The 12.5% rate belongs to tax years up to 2025. The reform also extended the carry-forward of tax losses and kept an additional 20% deduction for qualifying research and development expenditure incurred in 2025 to 2030. Section 33 applies the arm's length principle to related-party transactions and section 33Γ allows advance pricing agreements, which is the legal framework behind transfer pricing.

### Personal income tax from the 2026 tax year

The Second Schedule sets the individual bands as follows: 0% on the first €22,000; 20% from €22,000 to €32,000; 25% from €32,000 to €42,000; 30% from €42,000 to €72,000; and 35% on every euro above €72,000. The reform added section 14B with personal deductions for families below stated income thresholds, such as deductions per child and for mortgage interest or rent, and an 8% rate on gains from crypto-assets under section 20Ε.

Two exemptions matter for people who move to Cyprus for work. Section 8(23A) exempts 50% of remuneration from employment in Cyprus for 17 tax years where the person was not resident in Cyprus for at least 15 consecutive years before first employment here, the employment started on or after 1 January 2022 and the remuneration exceeds €55,000 in the year. Section 8(21A) exempts 20% of remuneration, capped at €8,550, for seven years for employment starting up to and including 2027. The firm checks the conditions against the employment contract and the client's travel history before a claim is made.

### Non-domiciled status and the Special Contribution for the Defence

SDC is a separate charge under the Special Contribution for the Defence Law 117(I)/2002 on dividends, interest and, historically, rents. For an individual it applies only if the person is both Cyprus tax resident under the Income Tax Law and domiciled in Cyprus. An individual with a domicile of origin outside Cyprus is therefore outside SDC, which is the so-called non-dom position. Section 2(3) provides that a person who has been resident for at least 17 of the 20 years before the tax year is deemed to have acquired Cyprus domicile, and keeps it until 20 years of non-residence have passed.

The reform added section 3Δ, which is new. A person without a Cyprus domicile of origin who becomes deemed domiciled under the 17-of-20 rule may elect an alternative method: a flat annual SDC of €50,000 regardless of income, paid as a single instalment of €250,000 covering a five-year period. The election is irrevocable for the five years, must be applied for by 30 June of the first year, requires acceptance by the Commissioner, is non-refundable and cannot be offset or credited against other taxes or foreign tax. It can be used for at most two five-year periods. This is an in-force provision in the consolidated law as at October 2026, not a proposal.

### Dividends and interest

Dividends are exempt from income tax and taxed, where at all, under SDC. Section 3 of the SDC law now sets the rate for resident and domiciled individuals at 5% of the gross dividend. A transitional rule applies 17% to dividends paid out of profits of tax years up to and including 2025 and received within six years of the amending law taking effect. Section 3Γ, the deemed distribution rule, now covers only profits of tax years 2024 and 2025: 70% of the after-tax profits of those years are deemed distributed two years after the year end and attract 17% SDC in the hands of resident and domiciled shareholders. Profits earned from 2026 onward are not deemed distributed. The reform also introduced a 10% SDC on disguised dividends, meaning the private use of company assets by a shareholder, reflected in the cross-reference in section 33 of the Income Tax Law.

Interest received by an individual who is resident and domiciled is subject to SDC at 17% of the gross amount under section 3B, reduced to 3% for interest on Cyprus or other EU government savings certificates and development bonds and on listed corporate bonds of local authorities and state bodies. The consolidated SDC law no longer contains a charge on rental income; professional summaries of the reform confirm that rents are taxed under income tax only from 2026.

### Intellectual property and the deduction on new capital

Section 9(1)(κ) of the Income Tax Law, the IP regime, allows 80% of the qualifying profits from a qualifying intangible asset to be deducted as a deemed expense, with the method of calculation set by regulations of the Council of Ministers; the taxpayer may waive the deduction in whole or in part each year. Section 9B grants a deduction on new equity introduced into a company, calculated at the yield of the ten-year government bond of the state in which the capital is invested plus five percentage points, and capped at 80% of the taxable income generated by the new capital. Both are in the consolidated law in October 2026. Both are also provisions that tax authorities scrutinise closely, so the firm documents the facts behind any claim.

### VAT

The VAT Law 95(I)/2000 sets the standard rate at 19% under section 17, a reduced rate of 5% under section 18 for supplies in the Fifth Schedule, and a reduced rate of 9% under section 18A for supplies in the Twelfth Schedule. The schedules are amended by decree, so the rate on a specific transaction, for instance the purchase of a new home, short-term rentals or cross-border services, is checked against the schedule in force on the date of supply. Registration obligations depend on the thresholds in the First Schedule.

### Capital gains tax on Cyprus immovable property

The Capital Gains Tax Law 52/1980 charges 20% on gains from the disposal of immovable property situated in Cyprus and of shares in companies that hold such property where at least 20% of the market value of the shares derives from it. Individuals have exemptions of €30,000 on gains in general, €50,000 for agricultural land disposed of by a farmer and €150,000 for a principal private residence occupied for at least five years, with only the larger of the exemptions available. These figures were raised by the reform. Gains on property outside Cyprus are outside the charge.

### Double tax treaties

Cyprus has a wide treaty network. The official list, with the text of each agreement and its protocols, is published by the Ministry of Finance; Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Poland, Russia and Ukraine are all on it. Section 34 of the Income Tax Law gives effect to the treaties and section 36 provides unilateral relief for foreign tax where no treaty applies. Because several treaties have been amended by protocol, the firm works from the treaty text and the protocol actually in force rather than from a summary.

### Stamp duty

The Stamp Duty Law 19/1963 was repealed by Law 239(I)/2025. Contracts, share transfers and loan agreements signed from 1 January 2026 no longer carry stamp duty.

## Tax audits, objections and appeals

The Tax Department can request information, examine records and raise assessments. The procedure for disputing an assessment is in the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law 4/1978. A written objection must reach the Commissioner within 60 days of service of the assessment notice; if the notice was served in December, by the end of the following February. The objector files the return if it has not been filed and pays the tax due on their own computation. The Commissioner must decide within three years of receiving the information requested, failing which the assessment is amended to the taxpayer's figures.

Against the Commissioner's decision, section 20A offers two routes: a hierarchical recourse to the Tax Council within 45 days of notification, which the Council may extend for absence from Cyprus, illness or other reasonable cause, or a judicial recourse under Article 146 of the Constitution. A decision of the Tax Council can in turn be challenged under section 21. The reform also changed the limitation period for assessments and the filing calendar; the firm confirms the dates that apply to each year before advising on timing.

This is the part of tax work that is squarely legal. Kalopetrides Law LLC prepares the objection with the supporting documents, argues the legal position before the Commissioner and the Tax Council, and litigates before the administrative courts. Where a criminal or administrative penalty is threatened, the firm's litigation practice and its external associate for civil and criminal litigation handle it.

## What this means for a foreign client

For an entrepreneur from Germany, Austria or the Netherlands, the 15% rate removes the old argument that a Cyprus company was taxed at an unusually low rate, and shifts attention to residence, substance and the treaty. For an individual relocating under the 60-day rule, the decisive questions are the permanent home, the employment or office, and what the home country will say. For a non-dom with dividend income, the 5% SDC rate for domiciled residents narrows the gap, but the exclusion from SDC remains the central benefit until the 17-of-20 threshold is reached, after which section 3Δ is the option to assess. For Russian-speaking and Ukrainian clients, the treaties with Russia and Ukraine remain on the Ministry's list, with protocols, and sanctions and banking compliance now sit alongside the tax analysis.
