Kalopetrides Law LLC is in the centre of Larnaca, acts for clients from Germany, Israel, Lebanon and other countries, and works in English, Greek, German and Russian.

## Where we are

The office is at Gladstonos 1, in the Panayioteio Megaro building, second floor, Office 205, a short walk from the centre of Larnaca and the seafront. Larnaca is the main entry point to Cyprus for most foreign visitors, with the island's principal international airport a few kilometres from the town, so clients arriving from abroad can be at the office quickly after landing.

Larnaca has its own District Court, District Lands Office and district offices of the main public authorities. For a property transfer, a residence application or a tax registration, being on the spot matters: documents are lodged in person, appointments are made locally and questions from an officer are answered the same day.

Buyers from outside the European Union have additional requirements to meet, and all buyers need the contract checked, the title and encumbrances searched and the contract deposited at the Land Registry. **Companies in the port and airport area.** Larnaca has a commercial port and the island's main airport, and many logistics, trading, hospitality and service businesses operate in the district. **Clients dealing with banks.** Opening and keeping a bank account, loan and security documentation, and disputes with a lender are common reasons foreign clients call a lawyer in Cyprus. See [banking and finance lawyer in Cyprus](/services/banking-finance-lawyer-cyprus/).

## How a matter typically runs

1. **First contact.** You call, email or message us and describe the matter in a few lines. We tell you whether it is something we handle, what documents we need and what the first step is.
2. **First consultation.** At the office or by video call. We explain how the procedure works in Cyprus, what the authorities will require and what the realistic timeline is.
3. **Engagement.** We confirm the scope of work and the basis of our fees in writing before we start.
4. **Execution.** We prepare and lodge the documents, deal with the Land Registry, the Registrar of Companies, the migration authority, the bank or the Tax Department, and keep you informed at each stage.
5. **Completion.** You receive the deed, the certificate, the permit or the decision, with an explanation of what to do next and what ongoing obligations you have in Cyprus.

## Working with us from abroad

Most of our foreign clients are not in Cyprus for most of the matter. That is normal and it works:

- Consultations are held by video call in English, Greek, German or Russian.
- Documents are exchanged by email, and where Cyprus law allows it, signed electronically or under a power of attorney so that you do not have to travel for every signature.
- Where a signature in person or an appearance before an authority is required, we tell you in advance so that one trip to Larnaca covers everything.
- For a property purchase, a power of attorney certified in your home country, with an apostille where required, lets us complete the transaction on your behalf.

## Why a local lawyer rather than an agent or a developer's lawyer

In a property purchase, the developer's or agent's lawyer acts for the developer or agent. An independent lawyer checks the title, the planning permits, the encumbrances and the contract on your behalf, and deposits the contract at the Land Registry to protect your position. The same applies to a company formation or a residence application: the person advising you should have no interest in the outcome other than yours.
