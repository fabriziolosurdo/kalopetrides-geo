TrustGate Corporate Services Ltd is the corporate services company affiliated with Kalopetrides Law LLC. It provides company administration and nominee services to businesses and international clients operating through Cyprus companies. Working in close coordination with the law firm, TrustGate delivers structured, compliant and confidential corporate support for entrepreneurs, corporate groups, holding structures and investment vehicles.

## Services

### Company formation and structuring

- Incorporation of Cyprus companies
- Establishment of branches and representative offices
- Support with corporate structuring and reorganisation
- Preparation and filing of statutory documentation

### Nominee services

- Provision of nominee directors
- Provision of nominee shareholders
- Corporate representation services
- Safekeeping of corporate documentation

All nominee arrangements are structured in accordance with applicable laws and regulatory requirements, with transparency, proper documentation and full compliance with anti-money-laundering (AML) obligations.

### Corporate administration

- Registered office facilities
- Company secretarial services
- Maintenance of statutory registers
- Filings with the Cyprus Registrar of Companies
- Ongoing corporate compliance monitoring

### Governance and compliance support

- Board administration support
- Preparation of corporate resolutions
- Beneficial-owner (UBO) reporting and regulatory filings
- Assistance with substance and governance requirements

## How TrustGate works

TrustGate Corporate Services Ltd places strong emphasis on regulatory compliance, confidentiality and operational efficiency. All services are delivered under due-diligence procedures and in accordance with the applicable Cyprus and EU regulatory framework.

The integrated structure with Kalopetrides Law LLC means that clients receive coordinated legal and corporate support: the law firm advises on the transaction or structure, and TrustGate implements and administers it, so that a company formation, a reorganisation or an ongoing compliance programme is handled from one place.

## Why Cyprus

Cyprus is a common choice for entrepreneurs and corporate groups establishing structures that are commercially sound, legally compliant and operationally efficient. The main reasons clients give are:

- membership of the European Union;
- a transparent corporate tax regime;
- a legal system based on common law;
- an extensive network of double-tax treaties;
- efficient corporate administration through the Registrar of Companies.
