Marianna Giannarou is responsible for corporate administration and compliance at Kalopetrides Law LLC. She is not a lawyer; she coordinates the administrative and regulatory procedures behind the firm's work for companies and private clients.

## Background

She holds academic qualifications in Human Resource Management and Business Administration from the University of Central Lancashire Cyprus, where she graduated with distinction. She has developed extensive professional experience in corporate administration, operational management, compliance and client relations through senior administrative and managerial roles across a range of industries in Cyprus and abroad. Working closely with multinational companies and international business operations across the Middle East, Africa and Australia, she has gained exposure to cross-border corporate environments, international business practices and multicultural client relations.

## Experience

Throughout her career she has been involved in overseeing corporate operations, coordinating administrative and regulatory procedures, supporting executive management and ensuring compliance with internal policies, labour legislation and operational requirements. Her experience includes managing international operations across African markets, handling corporate and administrative processes, coordinating recruitment and employee relations, maintaining relationships with clients and business partners, and supporting the day-to-day running of corporate functions in demanding business environments.

## How she works

With a strong organisational and problem-solving approach, she is recognised for managing complex administrative matters with professionalism, discretion and efficiency. Her practical understanding of corporate procedures, combined with a client-focused philosophy and attention to detail, allows her to provide reliable support to companies and private clients and to keep each stage of a process clear and well coordinated.

Committed to professionalism, integrity and long-term client relationships, she takes pride in delivering personalised and dependable support tailored to the specific needs of each client.
