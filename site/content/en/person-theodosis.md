Theodosis Kokozides is responsible for operations and corporate administration at Kalopetrides Law LLC. He is not a lawyer; his role is to keep the firm's processes and corporate administration running smoothly for clients.

## Background

He holds academic qualifications in Hospitality and Human Resources from Canterbury University and has built an international career through professional experience in Cyprus and Germany. Having lived and worked abroad for several years, he has held roles as Operations Manager and Administrator for several companies, gaining in-depth knowledge of corporate administration, real estate management and operational oversight.

## Experience with clients

Throughout his career he has worked closely with companies and private clients, supporting them in identifying, reviewing and evaluating business and real estate opportunities. His practical, hands-on approach helps clients move through complex processes with clarity and efficiency, and make informed decisions at each stage.

## How he works

He places a strong emphasis on trust, reliability and a client-focused way of working, and is committed to quality, professionalism and long-term client relationships, providing support tailored to each client's needs.
