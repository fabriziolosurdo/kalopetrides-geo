## What to prepare for a first consultation

A first consultation is more useful if you bring, or send in advance, the documents that relate to your matter. Depending on the subject, that usually means:

- **Property:** the reservation agreement or sale contract if you have one, the seller's or developer's details, and any title deed or plan you have received.
- **Company matters:** the certificate of incorporation and the company's statutory documents, the latest filings, and a description of what you want to do.
- **Immigration and residence:** your passport, your current residence or visa status in Cyprus, and evidence of income, employment or investment that supports the application.
- **Banking, finance and tax:** the agreements or correspondence in question, and your tax residence position.
- **Litigation:** the contract or document the dispute arises from, any correspondence, and any court papers you have received, with the dates.
- **Wills and estates:** any existing will, a list of assets in Cyprus and abroad, and the family details that matter for succession.

If you are unsure what is relevant, bring what you have. We will tell you what else is needed.
