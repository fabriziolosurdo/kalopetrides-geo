## Who we work with

Most of our clients come to Cyprus from abroad: private buyers purchasing a home or an investment property in the Larnaca district, investors and entrepreneurs setting up or acquiring a Cyprus company, families relocating and applying for residence, and businesses that need banking, finance or tax advice for their operations here. We also act for Cypriot companies and individuals.

The office works in English, Greek, German and Russian, so most clients can discuss their matter in a language they are comfortable with. Clients who live abroad can be advised by video call and can give instructions remotely; a visit to Larnaca is needed only where a signature in person or an appearance before an authority is required.
