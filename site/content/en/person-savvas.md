Savvas Kalopetrides is the founder and Managing Director of Kalopetrides Law LLC, a law firm in Larnaca, Cyprus. He holds an LLB (Hons) in Law from the University of Reading and is an active member of the Cyprus Bar Association.

## Practice

He advises on real estate law, corporate law, immigration, banking and finance, commercial law and tax advising, and handles civil and criminal litigation. His clients include private individuals, multinational companies and institutions, from Cyprus and abroad.

## How he works

Over the course of his career he has built a reputation for reliable service to both domestic and international clients, who rely on his strategic guidance and on legal services tailored to their situation. For cross-border matters he works with law firms in other countries, so that clients receive effective, practical solutions that fit their needs rather than a one-size-fits-all answer.

He is known for a client-centred approach: not only legal advice, but a personal working relationship. He places a high value on building lasting relationships with clients and continues to act as a trusted advisor on the complex matters they bring to the firm.
