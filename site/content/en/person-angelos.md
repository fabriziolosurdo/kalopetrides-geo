Angelos Ioannou is an external associate of Kalopetrides Law LLC. He is a lawyer specialising in civil and criminal litigation, with particular experience in real estate disputes, insurance claims, debt recovery and general civil matters. He holds an LLB in Law from the University of Reading and is a member of the Cyprus Bar Association.

## Practice

He has extensive experience in resolving a wide range of legal disputes, including conflicts between developers, purchasers and tenants, as well as matters relating to unpaid debts and insurance claims. He handles general civil litigation, advising clients involved in contract disputes, personal injury claims and other civil matters.

## How he works

With a thorough understanding of both civil and criminal law, he takes a strategic, client-focused approach to complex legal issues, working towards the best achievable outcome for the client in each case.
