Kalopetrides Law LLC is a law firm based in Larnaca, Cyprus. We advise private individuals, companies and organisations, from Cyprus and from abroad, on real estate, corporate and commercial, immigration, banking and finance, tax, wills and probate, intellectual property, litigation and civil marriage matters. The firm is led by its founder and Managing Director, lawyer Savvas Kalopetrides.

## Our approach

We work from a close knowledge of Cypriot law and practice, combined with an understanding of what clients from other countries expect. Most of the people who instruct us are buying property, setting up or running a company, relocating, or dealing with a bank or the tax authorities in a jurisdiction they do not know. Our job is to explain what happens in Cyprus, step by step, and to carry the matter through.

Three principles define how we work:

- **Practical advice grounded in experience.** We advise private individuals, companies and institutions on the matters that actually come up in Cyprus, and we give answers you can act on.
- **Local presence, cross-border reach.** The office is in Larnaca. For matters that touch other jurisdictions we work with a network of trusted associate lawyers and partners abroad, so that cross-border transactions and disputes can be coordinated from one place.
- **A client-first philosophy.** We treat each case with care, keep you informed, and give you strategic advice and effective representation rather than a standard product.

He advises on real estate, corporate, immigration, banking and finance, commercial and tax matters and handles civil and criminal litigation. The firm works with [Angelos Ioannou](/team/angelos-ioannou/), an external associate lawyer who specialises in civil and criminal litigation, including real estate disputes, insurance claims and debt recovery. He is a member of the Cyprus Bar Association.

Day-to-day operations and corporate administration are handled by [Theodosis Kokozides](/team/theodosis-kokozides/), whose background is in operations management and corporate administration in Cyprus and Germany, and by [Marianna Giannarou](/team/marianna-giannarou/), who looks after corporate administration and compliance.

## TrustGate Corporate Services Ltd

Corporate administration is provided through our affiliated company, [TrustGate Corporate Services Ltd](/trustgate-corporate-services/): company formation, registered office, company secretarial services, nominee directors and shareholders, maintenance of statutory registers and filings with the Registrar of Companies. Working alongside the law firm, TrustGate allows legal and corporate services to be coordinated under one roof.
