A brand, a design or a piece of software is worth protecting only if the protection is actually registered and enforced where you trade. Kalopetrides Law LLC registers trademarks and designs for businesses in Cyprus and abroad, advises on EU-wide protection through EUIPO, drafts licensing and distribution agreements, and acts against infringement before the Registrar and the Cyprus courts.

## How it works in Cyprus

**National trademarks.** Trademarks in Cyprus are governed by the Trademarks Law, Cap. 268, as amended by the Trade Marks (Amendment) Laws of 2020 and 2021. The register of trademarks is kept by the Intellectual Property Section of the Department of Registrar of Companies and Intellectual Property, which is part of the Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry. Applications are filed, and most procedures are carried out, through the Department's electronic services.

**Examination.** The Section first checks the formal requirements of the application and the classification of the goods and services under the Nice Classification; deficiencies must be corrected within two months. It then examines absolute grounds for refusal (whether the sign is registrable and has distinctive character) and relative grounds (whether it conflicts with earlier identical or similar trademarks). If the Registrar raises objections, the applicant has two months to withdraw the application, limit the goods or services, or file observations, and may in justified cases request a hearing. The Registrar's final decision can be appealed to the Administrative Court.

**Publication, opposition and registration.** Accepted applications are published in the Official Gazette. If no opposition is filed within three months of publication, or an opposition is dismissed, the Registrar proceeds to registration, publishes it in the Gazette and issues the registration certificate. Before expiry the Section sends the owner a renewal notice; if the renewal fee is not paid, the mark is published under unpaid renewal fees and, after a further two months, struck off the register, subject to restoration on payment of the fees and an affidavit explaining the delay.

**EU trade marks.** A business that trades in several EU countries can file a single application with the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO) for an EU trade mark, which gives exclusive rights in all EU Member States. According to EUIPO, an EU trade mark registration lasts for 10 years and can be renewed indefinitely. A national Cyprus mark and an EU mark can coexist, and an EU mark that fails can in some cases be converted into national applications.

**International registration.** Protection in countries outside the EU can be sought through the Madrid System administered by WIPO, on the basis of a national or EU application or registration.

## For foreign businesses

Companies that set up in Cyprus to serve EU or regional markets usually need to decide quickly whether their brand should be protected in Cyprus only, across the EU, or in third countries as well. A clearance search before the application avoids the cost of an objection or opposition later. Where a brand is already registered in your home country, we check whether the Cyprus or EU application can claim priority and whether the existing specification of goods and services is suitable for the Cyprus market.
