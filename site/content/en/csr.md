At Kalopetrides Law LLC we are dedicated to making meaningful and lasting contributions to the communities we serve. Our commitment to social responsibility takes the form of active participation in local charitable foundations, community projects and corporate giving. These efforts are part of the firm's culture.

## Giving back to the community

The firm's lawyer, staff and associates support the local community through legal services, grassroots fundraising, donations and volunteering. We believe that giving back creates more opportunities for others, and that corporate responsibility is part of running a firm well.

## Ethical and sustainable practice

We are committed to conducting our business ethically and sustainably, with respect for people and for the environment. We have put in place initiatives intended to strengthen our community impact and encourage positive social change.

## Equality and inclusion

Our responsibility extends to upholding the firm's core values and mission, with a focus on promoting equality and inclusion for everyone we work with and for.
