We act as your strategic partners

With a great deal of experience in commercial law and international trade, our Commercial & Corporate team balance their thorough understanding and knowledge of law with pragmatic business strategies. Providing clear guidance, they apply their expertise across all forms of business transaction, from drafting agreements and negotiations, advising on tax law and code compliance, to intellectual property rights, bankruptcy proceedings and financial reporting. Naturally, the team can act as an advisor to executives or represent a corporation in court.

This highly capable team also deals with the formation and acquisition of companies, offering advice from start-up to the completion of incorporation, annual operation and dissolutions. They also provide clear guidance on issues arising from corporate structuring, transactions, mergers and acquisitions, shareholder agreements, joint ventures and project finance.
