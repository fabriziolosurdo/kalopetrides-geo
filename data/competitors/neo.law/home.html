Our Firm

The Largest Law Firm in Cyprus

Elias Neocleous & Co LLC is the largest law firm in Cyprus by a considerable margin and is generally recognised as the leading law firm in the South-East Mediterranean region. With more than 155 fee-earners operating across six offices, we offer unparalleled depth of expertise and jurisdictional knowledge.

Our attention to client service is what sets us apart. For us, the client comes first: we take pride in our responsiveness and in meeting deadlines and commitments. Our legal teams are comprised of first-in-class Cyprus lawyers and legal professionals from multidisciplinary backgrounds, ready to provide bespoke advice and legal services tailored to each client.

Situated at the crossroads of Europe, Asia, the Middle East and Africa, Cyprus offers a unique strategic advantage for international business and investment.
