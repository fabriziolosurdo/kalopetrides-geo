About Philippou Law Firm

Founded in 1984 in Paphos, Cyprus. For over 40 years our team has combined deep knowledge of Cypriot and EU law with a pragmatic, client-focused approach, helping individuals, entrepreneurs, and multinational corporations incorporate companies, relocate, purchase property, plan taxes, and resolve disputes.

Our practice areas span company formation and corporate structuring, immigration and permanent residency permits, tax planning under the Cyprus non-dom regime, real estate transactions, wills and probate, and civil litigation. Whether you are an entrepreneur setting up a Cyprus company or a family relocating to the island, we provide end-to-end legal support.

Tax Planning

Keep more of what you earn

Cyprus offers one of the most attractive tax regimes in Europe, making it an ideal jurisdiction for international businesses, investors, and high-net-worth individuals looking to optimise their tax position legally.

Tax and Accounting Services in CyprusTax Calculators
  • Standard 15% corporate tax rate from 2026
  • No withholding tax on dividends paid to non-residents
  • Non-domiciled (non-dom) regime: exemption from SDC on dividends and interest
  • Extensive double tax treaty network with 65+ countries
  • IP Box regime with effective tax rate as low as 3%
  • No succession or inheritance tax
  • Personal income tax: first €22,000 tax-free for residents
