Quick Answer

What is the Philippou Law Firm Partner Network?

The Partner Network is a structured way for advisers, firms and organisations to work with Philippou Law Firm and offer their clients or members Cyprus legal, tax and corporate services. There are three routes: Professional Partners deliver Cyprus services to their clients through our team, Referral Partners simply introduce clients and we take it from there, and Strategic and Ecosystem Partners offer our services as a benefit to a whole community. Every arrangement is set out in writing and complies with the professional rules for a regulated legal practice.

How it works

Three steps, whichever route you choose.

  1. 01

    Talk to our team

    Tell us about your clients or community and what you need in Cyprus. We run a quick conflict and compliance check.

  2. 02

    Choose the right partnership

    Professional, Referral, or Strategic and Ecosystem. We help you pick the route that fits how closely you want to stay involved.

  3. 03

    Onboard and start

    We put the arrangement in writing, give you what you need to present Cyprus, and start looking after your clients.

Why it works

A partnership built on services people genuinely need.

Recurring Cyprus services

Relocation and residency permits, tax residency and Non-Dom registration, company formation and ongoing administration, accounting, tax compliance and the tax work itself. Much of it renews year after year, not one off tasks.

Complementary to your work

A UK tax adviser handles the UK side, from tax deregistration to exit tax planning, while we handle the Cyprus side: residency permits, tax residency and Non-Dom registration, tax planning in Cyprus and company registration. We fit alongside you, not against you.

Cyprus is an international market

Most clients who move to or set up in Cyprus start with an adviser abroad. A structured programme turns occasional introductions into a reliable channel.
