The Cyprus Legal System

Cyprus has a common law legal system, inherited from the British period, which makes it particularly accessible for English speaking individuals and businesses. The legal framework is closely aligned with English common law principles, offering familiarity and predictability for international clients.

As a member of the European Union since 2004, Cyprus has also adopted EU directives and regulations across key areas including corporate governance, data protection, immigration and financial services. This dual foundation, a common law tradition combined with EU regulatory standards, makes Cyprus an attractive jurisdiction for cross border business, investment and relocation.

At Philippou Law Firm, our lawyers have extensive experience navigating both the Cypriot common law system and the wider EU legal framework. Whether you are forming a company, purchasing property, applying for residency or resolving a dispute, we provide the expert legal guidance you need.
