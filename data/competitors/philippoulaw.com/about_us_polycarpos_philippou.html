About Polycarpos

Polycarpos Philippou is the Founding Partner of Philippou Law Firm, with extensive experience in litigation, criminal law, administrative law, and civil law. A graduate of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (LL.B., 1979), Polycarpos has built a distinguished career focused on complex legal disputes. Fluent in Greek and English, he is a respected member of the Cyprus Bar Association and brings knowledge to every case.
