OUR FIRM

Patrikios Legal

Patrikios Legal is a leading Cyprus law firm, renowned for its top-tiered and multi-awarded  legal services and professional Cyprus lawyers.

The firm is located in Limassol, one of the most attractive and rapidly developing cities in Europe. It is driven by the increasing demand for legal services and its persistent commitment in providing top quality, customer-oriented and commercially-aware legal services.

Today, over 30 professional associates and legal consultants, as well as over 30 skilled paralegals, other qualified and motivated individuals greatly contribute to the legal needs of clients and associates from all over the world.
