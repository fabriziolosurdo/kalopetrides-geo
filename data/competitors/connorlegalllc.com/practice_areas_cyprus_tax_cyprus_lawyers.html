Tax

How We Can Help

Tax law can be a complex and ever-changing landscape, making it essential to have expert guidance to ensure compliance with the Cyprus Tax department and optimize your tax position. At Connor Legal, our Tax legal services provide comprehensive support for individuals and businesses navigating the intricacies of Cyprus tax law. Our team of experienced tax attorneys combines in-depth knowledge of local and international tax regulations with practical insights, delivering tailored advice and representation that helps our clients achieve their financial goals.

Our Tax legal services cover a wide range of areas.

helping you in Tax issues

Tax lawyers in Cyprus

International Tax

Our tax attorneys offer expert advice on international tax matters, including cross-border transactions, tax treaties, transfer pricing, and international tax planning.

Tax Compliance & Reporting

We assist clients with tax compliance and reporting obligations, ensuring that they meet all legal requirements and avoid potential penalties.

Tax Incentives & Reliefs

Our team provides guidance on available tax incentives and reliefs, helping clients take advantage of these opportunities to optimize their tax position.

Tax Planning & Structuring

We provide strategic tax planning and structuring advice for businesses and individuals, helping clients minimize their tax liabilities and optimize their tax position in compliance with local and international regulations.

Corporate Tax

Our tax attorneys assist businesses with all aspects of corporate taxation, including tax-efficient structuring, mergers and acquisitions, cross-border transactions, and tax compliance.

Personal Tax

We offer expert guidance on personal tax matters for individuals, including income tax, capital gains tax, inheritance tax, and tax planning for high-net-worth individuals.

VAT & Indirect Taxation

Our team advises clients on VAT and other indirect tax matters, ensuring that they comply with all relevant regulations and minimize their tax liabilities.

Tax Disputes & Litigation

We represent clients in tax disputes and litigation, providing robust advocacy and strategic counsel to achieve favorable outcomes in negotiations, administrative proceedings, and court cases.
