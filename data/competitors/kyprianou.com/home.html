Awards

Michael Kyprianou & Co LLC has been awarded the Cyprus Firm of the Year Award by Who’s Who Legal (WWL). Recognized internationally by Who’s Who Legal as Cyprus’ leading firm, Michael Kyprianou Law Firm has achieved 13 recommended lawyer listings in 2023 across eight practice areas including Arbitration, Asset Recovery, Commercial Litigation, Corporate Tax, Professional Negligence, Real Estate, and Restructuring & Insolvency, and additional Thought Leader listings in the practice areas of Commercial Litigation and Private Client.

Michael Kyprianou & Co LLC is ranked as one of the leading law firms in Cyprus in the  Chambers and Partners Europe 2023 Guide in both categories, General Business Law and Dispute Resolution, for the third consecutive year. “Michael Kyprianou & Co. LLC’s client service is exceptional, targeted to serve the best interests of the client, always practical, straightforward and on point.”

The firm has been ranked Tier 1 for Dispute Resolution by The Legal 500 2023 EMEA guide. The Dispute Resolution team is described as “very strong, capable of handling the most complex disputes and is one of the best offerings in Cyprus combining traditional advice with innovative solutions.”

The firm’s Banking & Finance and Corporate/M&A Departments have been highly recognised by IFLR1000 for their services and expertise. Client feedback confirms the Banking and Finance team’s “Creative thinking, flexible approach, reasonable pricing, meeting tight deadlines.” and highlights the client-oriented service approach by the M&A team providing “Very thorough and professional analysis and also recommendations for covering the company for future issues with the law.”

Michael Kyprianou & Co LLC  has been ranked as a Tier 1 law firm by The Legal 500 for Dispute Resolution, Commercial, Corporate and M&A, Employment, Intellectual Property and Real Estate & Construction, and achieved further recognition as a highly regarded and ranked firm in the practices of Banking and Finance, EU and Competition, Maritime and Admiralty Law, and Tax Law. A total of 17 lawyers of the firm have received individual recommendations by The Legal 500 for their outstanding expertise.

Michael Kyprianou & Co LLC has been named second largest leading law firm in Cyprus, maintaining its position for 2023 in the “The Legal Issue” of GOLD, the business magazine of Cyprus. The edition features 90+ law firms, that are known to be making a significant contribution to the sector, listed according to the number of lawyers employed.
