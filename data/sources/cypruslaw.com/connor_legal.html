About

Connor Legal boasts a long and distinguished history within the Cypriot legal sector. Throughout the years, the firm has adapted to the evolving demands of its clients, all while upholding the core values that have established its reputation as a trusted name in the Cypriot legal community.

We have a proven track record and continuously work towards effectively representing our clients.

Our areas of expertise include Commercial, Corporate, Dispute Resolution, Personal Injury, and Real Estate & Construction. The services offered by our Cyprus Law Firm encompass legal advice, representation in court hearings, drafting legal documents, and acting as counsel in International Commercial Arbitrations.
