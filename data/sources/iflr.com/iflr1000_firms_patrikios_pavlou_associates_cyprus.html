Firm overview

With a remarkable legacy, Patrikios Legal proudly celebrates 62 years of delivering exceptional legal services. Established in 1963 our firm has evolved into one of Cyprus' largest and most successful law firms.

We are a top-tier, multi award winning law firm, with significant expertise in the local and international legal marker, offering services in a wide spectrum of practice areas. Throughout our journey, we have consistently demonstrated unwavering commitment, exceptional service, and dedication to our clients. Our team of proficient lawyers specializes in providing comprehensive legal solutions for national and international matters. With an esteemed network of associates and a strong client portfolio worldwide, we have earned numerous accolades and recognition for our top-tier services.

As we mark this significant milestone, we reaffirm our pledge to continue delivering unparalleled legal services, innovation, and professionalism and to actively contribute to society. Our core principles are the provision of continuing value, quality, respect, integrity and professionalism towards our clients and associates. Our 62-year journey is a testament to our unwavering pursuit of excellence in the ever-evolving legal landscape.

Patrikios Pavlou & Associates
Linked Reviews
Financial and corporate
Patrikios Pavlou & Associates has established a strong position in the Cypriot market through its work across banking and finance, corporate transactions, restructuring and cross-border advisory matters. The firm is particularly recognised for its lender-side work, regularly advising financial institutions, investment vehicles and international businesses on financing arrangements, restructurings and strategic corporate matters.

The practice maintains a broad corporate and finance offering, acting for domestic and international clients on acquisitions, financing transactions, commercial arrangements and investment structures. The team is frequently involved in matters with cross-border elements, where Cyprus law expertise is required alongside international transactional and financing work.

Recent work reflects the firm's continued activity across financial services, fiduciary services, restructuring and corporate advisory mandates. Its client base includes Rengton Enterprises Limited, Revita Turnarounds Limited, Chabrier Avocats and Crystalserve Fiduciary Services Limited, demonstrating the firm's strength in advising financial, corporate and professional services clients operating in Cyprus and internationally.

Clients value the firm's combination of technical expertise and commercial judgement, particularly on cross-border corporate and financing matters. Feedback highlights the team's ability to provide practical advice, coordinate effectively across jurisdictions and deliver solutions that balance legal complexity with commercial objectives. The practice is also recognised for its expertise in private wealth-related structures and its ability to support clients on sophisticated international matters.
