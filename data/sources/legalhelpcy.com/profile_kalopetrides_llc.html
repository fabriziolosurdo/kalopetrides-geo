About Us

Kalopetrides LLC is a distinguished law firm providing exceptional legal services across a wide range of practice areas. Our firm has built a reputation for excellence, offering expert advice and personalized legal solutions to individuals, businesses, and organizations both locally and internationally.

With a deep understanding of Cypriot law and a global perspective, we are committed to delivering tailored legal services that meet the unique needs of each client. Our commitment to professional excellence is at the heart of everything we do, ensuring that we consistently provide the highest standards of legal advice and representation.

Our team is supported by a network of trusted external associates and partners around the world, enabling us to provide seamless, cross-border legal support in all matters. Whether you are dealing with real estate transactions, navigating the complexities of corporate law, seeking tax advice, or addressing matters of immigration, our team has the expertise to guide you with confidence.

People

Mr. Savvas Kalopetrides is the visionary founder and Managing Director of Kalopetrides LLC, a leading law firm offering expert legal counsel across a broad spectrum of sectors. He holds an LLB (Hons) in Law from the University of Reading and is an active member of the Cyprus Bar Association. He specializes in Real Estate Law, Corporate Law, Immigration, Banking and Finance, Commercial Law, Tax Advising as well as Civil and Criminal Litigation, providing sophisticated legal solutions to a wide array of clients.

Throughout his career, Mr. Kalopetrides has built a reputation for delivering unparalleled service to both domestic and international clients. His distinguished clientele includes private individuals, multinational corporations, and institutions, all of whom benefit from his strategic guidance and tailored legal services. By collaborating with prominent law firms around the world, he ensures that his clients receive effective and practical solutions aligned with their specific needs.

Known for his client-centric approach, Mr. Kalopetrides is dedicated to providing not only expert legal counsel but also a personalized experience. With a focus on building lasting relationships, he continues to be a trusted advisor, delivering innovative legal solutions that meet the complex needs of his clients.

Mr. Angelos Ioannou is a distinguished external associate of the firm, specializing in Civil and Criminal Litigation, with particular expertise in real estate disputes, insurance claims, debt recovery, and general civil matters. He holds an LLB in Law from the University of Reading and is a member of the Cyprus Bar Association.

Mr. Ioannou has extensive experience in resolving a wide range of legal disputes, including conflicts between developers, purchasers, and tenants, as well as issues related to unpaid debts and insurance claims. He is adept at handling general civil litigation matters, providing expert legal counsel to clients involved in contract disputes, personal injury claims, and other civil matters.

With a thorough understanding of both civil and criminal law, Mr. Ioannou employs a strategic, client-focused approach to navigate complex legal issues and ensure favorable outcomes.
