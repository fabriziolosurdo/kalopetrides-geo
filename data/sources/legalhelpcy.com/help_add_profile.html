How to add a lawyer or law firm profile

All help articles

Clients on LegalHelpCY are already looking for legal help in Cyprus — by city and area of law. A professional profile presents your practice at the moment of search: specialties, languages, office, contacts, and online booking. Enquiries come to you directly.

The same profile works for an individual lawyer and for a law firm: use your name or the firm’s name as the title.

Create an account first, then complete your profile. See How to register.

Get started

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open https://new.legalhelpcy.com/profile.
  3. Click Register now or Start registration.

Three steps to appear in the directory:

  1. Register — your account and essential contact details.
  2. Complete your profile — description, legal fields, languages, contacts, address, and availability.
  3. Get found — publish your profile so clients can find you and request a consultation.

Complete your profile

The form is organised in steps: Next, Back, and Save at the end. When you edit later, the same sections open as tabs. The more complete the card, the easier it is for a client to choose you.

Basic info

  • profile title (the page heading): the lawyer’s name or the firm’s name;
  • name in Russian, if it differs;
  • photo;
  • specialties;
  • city;
  • Services.

Contacts

  • office address;
  • map location;
  • phone;
  • WhatsApp and Viber;
  • visit format: online or in person.

About

  • description of your practice;
  • education and additional information;
  • consultation languages;
  • photo gallery.

Calendar

  • working hours so clients can pick a time on your page;
  • appointment duration (typically 30 minutes);
  • an external booking link if you use another calendar.

Publishing

Your profile is saved as a draft, reviewed, and then listed in the directory by city and specialty.

One account can hold several profiles — for example a personal practice and a firm. Add another with Add profile.

Once the profile is published, clients book from your page. How that looks for them: How to book a consultation.
