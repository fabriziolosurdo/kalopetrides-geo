Overview

Provided by Patrikios Legal

  • Senior & Managing Partner: Stavros Pavlou
    Number of partners: 10
    Number of lawyers: 30
    Languages: English, French, German, Greek, Italian, Russian, Slovak

    Firm Overview:
    Patrikios Pavlou & Associates LLC is a leading law firm in Cyprus, highly recommended for its expertise in multiple practice areas and recognised as a top performer in respect of its distinctive, professional client services and legal advice. Backed up with nearly 60 years of experience in the local and international legal market, the firm handles cases in conventional and alternative forms of dispute resolution, including arbitration and mediation in Cyprus and abroad. The team has been involved in some of the largest cross-border banking, financial and corporate transactions. Clients include public and private companies from various industry sectors, local and international banks, financial institutions, entrepreneurs as well as a host of individual clients from Europe, Asia, the USA, Russia, the CIS countries, and Cyprus, amongst others.

    Patrikios Pavlou & Associates LLC is a member of important professional international bodies and it has established an esteemed network of associates worldwide. Through Pagecorp Group, a group of companies associated through common ownership, the firm also provides a full range of corporate services and advises on International Tax Planning, Cyprus International Trusts, Foreign Investment and Citizenship, and Accounting and Financial reporting.

    Dispute Resolution & ADR:
    7 partners, 21 fee earners
    The traditionally strong dispute resolution department, undisputedly acknowledged in complex cases of all types and across the border, has recently handled cases such as:
    ■ Handling more than 60 cross-border cases, with an aggregate amount in excess of USD 3 billion, of one of the leading banks in Russia, involving the registration, recognition and enforcement of Russian judgements and arbitral awards
    ■ Providing legal advice to a multinational telecommunications group in relation to the enforcement of foreign judgements for USD 4 billion by promoting a civil claim of fraud against the judgement debtors
    ■ Advising the London office of the largest litigation and arbitration law firm in the world in arbitration proceedings before the LCIA in relation to a claim worth USD 200 million
    ■ Representing the client in a civil case filed against him and other entities before the Dubai International Financial Centre (value USD 35 million)
