Description

Expert Lawyers in Paphos for Your Cyprus Legal Needs

For over 40 years, Philippou Law has been a leading law firm in Paphos, providing trusted legal counsel across Cyprus. Our experienced team combines a strong legacy with a modern, practical approach to property, immigration, corporate, and tax law.

With a presence dating back to 1980, we offer authoritative guidance and reliable solutions tailored to each client’s needs. We pride ourselves on clear communication, personalised service, and handling every legal matter with care and precision.

We specialise in supporting international clients, expatriates, and investors, offering comprehensive legal assistance for residency, business setup, and property matters in Cyprus.
