Pricing questions

Is the listing really free?

Yes. Every law firm gets a free, verified directory profile — listed across the relevant city and practice-area pages, with direct phone and email enquiries. There's no subscription and no card required to list.

So how do you make money?

Boosts. When you want more visibility you can pay a one-off fee to lift your listing to the top of a chosen category — a practice area or a city you cover — for a set number of days or weeks. It's entirely optional.

Why can't I add my website?

Listings keep enquiries direct: clients call or email your firm straight from your profile, rather than clicking away. It keeps the directory consistent and the leads yours — no middleman, no commission.

How does a boost work?

From your dashboard, pick a category you appear in and a duration. Your listing jumps to the top of that category and is marked “Promoted” for the whole window. When it ends, you simply return to the standard free listing — nothing recurring.

How are listings verified?

We check the firm's identity and Cyprus Bar Association membership before applying the Verified badge. Verification is free and separate from boosting.
