      <ul>
        <li data-i18n="priv.li1"><strong>Contact information:</strong> such as your name, job title, postal address, home address, business address, telephone number, mobile number, fax number, and email address.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.li2"><strong>Payment data:</strong> data necessary for us to process payments and implement fraud prevention measures, including card numbers, security codes, and billing details.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.li3"><strong>Business details:</strong> business information we process as part of our instructions or projects, or otherwise provided by you voluntarily.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.li4"><strong>Compliance details:</strong> information we are legally required to collect, such as "know your client" information, sanctions and restrictive measures details, and information about relevant litigation.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.li5"><strong>Preferences:</strong> information about your preferences, where relevant to the services we provide.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.li6"><strong>Platform information:</strong> your password and related log-in details for platforms maintained by us, where you have access.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.li7"><strong>Publicly available information:</strong> information from publicly available resources, including databases used for compliance checks or credit rating agencies.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.li8"><strong>Statutory register information:</strong> information about you on account of an interest or office you may hold in, or relationships with, a corporate entity, partnership, trust, or other vehicle to which we provide services (a "Third-Party Entity").</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.li9"><strong>Details for services:</strong> in some cases we may collect sensitive information (e.g., health-related data) to tailor our services, based on your consent.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2 data-i18n="priv.h3">When do we collect your personal data?</h2>
      <ul>
        <li data-i18n="priv.when1">When you or your organization seek our services.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.when2">When you make inquiries via our website, email, phone, or in person.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.when3">When a Third-Party Entity engages us, and you hold an office/interest in that entity.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.when4">When you or your organization provide services to us.</li>
      </ul>
      <p data-i18n="priv.h3p">We may also collect data from third parties (e.g., government agencies, credit reporting agencies, or public records).</p>

      <h2 data-i18n="priv.h4">How will we use your personal data?</h2>
      <p data-i18n="priv.h4p">We use your personal data for the following purposes (Permitted Purposes):</p>
      <ul>
        <li data-i18n="priv.use1">Managing business relationships (payments, accounting, audits).</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.use2">Legal compliance (anti-money laundering, fraud prevention, sanctions checks).</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.use3">Providing updates relevant to your role in a Third-Party Entity.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.use4">Improving services, communications, and security.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.use5">Defending legal rights or complying with court orders.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.use6">Marketing (e.g., newsletters, events) with your consent.</li>
      </ul>
      <p data-i18n="priv.h4p2">Legal bases for processing: contractual necessity, legal obligations, legitimate interests, or your consent.</p>

      <h2 data-i18n="priv.h5">How will we share your personal data?</h2>
      <ul>
        <li data-i18n="priv.share1">With Third-Party Entities for administrative or billing purposes.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.share2">With third-party service providers (e.g., compliance screening, IT support) under strict confidentiality.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.share3">With courts, regulators, or law enforcement authorities as required.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.share4">As aggregated data for website analytics (no individual identification).</li>
      </ul>

      <h2 data-i18n="priv.h6">Can you refuse to share your personal data with us?</h2>
      <p data-i18n="priv.h6p">You may withhold data, but this may limit our ability to provide services (e.g., compliance checks).</p>

      <h2 data-i18n="priv.h7">How do we keep your personal data safe?</h2>
      <p data-i18n="priv.h7p">We implement technical and organizational measures (e.g., secure systems, restricted access) to protect your data.</p>

      <h2 data-i18n="priv.h8">Personal data we receive from you about other people</h2>
      <p data-i18n="priv.h8p">You must ensure individuals whose data you share with us are aware of this Privacy Policy.</p>

      <h2 data-i18n="priv.h9">How long do we keep your data?</h2>
      <p data-i18n="priv.h9p">We retain data only as long as necessary (e.g., legal obligations, claims defense).</p>

      <h2 data-i18n="priv.h10">What rights do you have?</h2>
      <ul>
        <li data-i18n="priv.right1">Access, correct, or delete your data.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.right2">Object to processing or restrict use.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.right3">Withdraw consent or opt out of marketing.</li>
        <li data-i18n="priv.right4">Lodge complaints via <a href="mailto:info@kalopetrideslaw.com">info@kalopetrideslaw.com</a> (proof of ID required).</li>
      </ul>

      <p data-i18n="priv.h11p">Notify us at <a href="mailto:info@kalopetrideslaw.com">info@kalopetrideslaw.com</a> of any changes.</p>
